Questions

FAQ

Where is the studio?
A quiet home-based studio in The Draycott condo, Orchard. The exact unit is shared after booking confirmation.
What should I wear and bring?
Any comfortable workout clothes are fine — form-fitting is ideal, as it helps me see and correct your posture more precisely. Grip socks are recommended, but regular socks are perfectly okay too. Please bring a towel and a water bottle.
Can I do sessions while pregnant?
Yes — prenatal Pilates is one of my specialisms. Sessions are adapted for every trimester. Please mention your stage when booking.
How do I pay?
Payment is via PayNow. A QR code is shown at the end of booking. Your slot is confirmed once payment is received, and we'll send a WhatsApp confirmation.
What is the cancellation policy?
Please contact us directly for any cancellations or changes. Late cancellations (under 24 hours) or no-shows are non-refundable.
